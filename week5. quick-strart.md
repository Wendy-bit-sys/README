# Quick start: create your first social media post

This guide is for small business owners and social media managers who are new to Canva. It covers one task: creating a social media post from a template. You do not need any design experience.

- **Time needed:** about 10 minutes
- **You will finish with:** one post ready to use

## Before you start

- A free Canva account (sign in at canva.com)
- A short piece of text, such as a product name, a price, or an event date
- One image of your own, if you want to use it instead of the sample photo

## Step 1 — Choose a template and set the size

1. Sign in, then click **Create a design** on the home page.
2. Pick **Social media**, then choose the size you need, for example **Instagram Post (Square)**.
3. In the templates panel on the left, browse or search for a style, such as "sale" or "announcement".
4. Click a template to place it on the canvas.

**Note:** choosing the right size here matters. Changing it later can move your text around.

## Step 2 — Replace the sample text

1. Double-click the headline text box and type your own headline.
2. Do the same for the smaller text, such as the price or the date.
3. Delete any sample words you do not need.

## Step 3 — Replace the sample image

1. Click the image on the canvas.
2. Use **Replace** in the toolbar, or drag a photo from your computer onto the canvas.
3. Drag the picture until the important part is visible.

## Step 4 — Check your work

Before you use the post, check that:

- [ ] All sample words (for example "Your headline here") are gone
- [ ] The headline is readable on a phone screen
- [ ] No text or image is cut off at the edges
- [ ] Prices, dates, and spelling are correct

## Step 5 — Save it

Canva saves your design automatically, so you can close the page and find it again under **Your projects** on the home page.
